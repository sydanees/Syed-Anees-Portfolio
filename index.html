Build an omni-channel framework for customer support

TL;DR

  • Six tools → one desk
  • 38% faster first reply
  • Live with 4,000 agents

Context

Freshworks' support suite had grown one channel at a time. Chat, email, voice, social and the knowledge base each had their own inbox, their own rules, and their own idea of what a conversation was.

I led design for the framework that pulled them together, working with 4 designers and 20 engineers across multiple regions, from discovery through rollout.

The problem

We shadowed agents over several weeks. A single refund question moved through six tools before anyone replied, and the average ticket touched six tabs. Agents were not slow — the desk was.

Explorations — four routes, one shipped

Split inboxParked

One pane per channel. Quick to build, but agents still carried the context across panes themselves.

ThreadedShipped

Every channel folded into one customer thread. Won on comprehension; we paid for it in density and had to earn the space back.

Queue-firstParked

Work the queue, never the inbox. Strong for high-volume teams, weak for named accounts that expect a relationship.

Agent copilotPhase two

Drafts and summaries beside the reply box. Held back until answer quality scored above target in testing.

What shipped

One conversation object behind every channel, a routing model teams can edit themselves, and an agent desk where history, macros and automation sit beside the reply box instead of behind it.
