About

I like people, problems and figuring things out. I'm a product designer working across SaaS, AI and conversational experiences. Over the last twelve years I've worked on everything from mobile apps to enterprise customer software, usually somewhere in the messy bit where nobody quite knows what the product should do yet.

These days I'm at Freshworks, designing AI Agents, Copilot, Conversational AIX and omnichannel experiences. I like working alongside people rather than simply designing for them, figuring things out together and turning ideas into experiences that feel right.

Outside of work, I scribble and chase good light with a camera.
